STARTER PROMPTS — paste each Title and Text into Agent Builder > Configure > Starter prompts (12 maximum).
Angle brackets are for the user to replace.

1. Title: What's in here?
   Text: Give me a short overview of this knowledge base: the three workbooks and their current versions, what each sheet holds, and how many records of each type there are.

2. Title: Policies for my research
   Text: Which UK policies and activities in the governance workbook could use data or research on <topic, e.g. soil moisture>? Screen every record, and give the Record_ID, name, why it is relevant and the link.

3. Title: Trace a governance chain
   Text: Trace the governance chain for <Record_ID or policy name>: enabling legislation, lead body, parent programme, funding, international links and indicator frameworks, stating each link's type.

4. Title: Climate-relevant indicators
   Text: Which indicators on <topic> take a climate input? Use Climate_Score and the measured-quantity test, and list framework, Record_ID, units and the climate rationale.

5. Title: Is this already held?
   Text: Is <policy, body, Act or indicator> already in the workbooks or in the pending-additions queue? Only count a record of the same kind, not one that merely mentions it.

6. Title: Start an applied assessment
   Text: I want to run an applied assessment of existing Met Office research on <field>. Ask me for the inputs you need, then follow the method and give me the YAML config.

7. Title: Start a gap assessment
   Text: I want to run a gap (prospective) assessment for a product the Met Office could build: <product>. Ask me for the inputs you need, then follow the method and give me the YAML config.

8. Title: Explain the scoring
   Text: Explain the two-axis scoring (Relevance and Status or Maturity), when to use applied versus gap mode, and give an example of a "3 / Opportunity" row.

9. Title: Render my config
   Text: I have a YAML config from you. Tell me where to save it, how to render the PDF and Word files, and what to check before I send the report.

10. Title: What changed recently?
    Text: What changed in the latest version of each workbook? Summarise the Changelog sheets, most recent first.

11. Title: Draft a new record
    Text: Draft a proposal for adding <record> to the right workbook and sheet: every column value, primary sources, confidence, and the other records it would link to. Leave blanks rather than guess.

12. Title: Pending queue status
    Text: Summarise the pending-additions queue: how many candidates are pending, decided or rejected, grouped by family, and which are blocked by an open schema decision.
