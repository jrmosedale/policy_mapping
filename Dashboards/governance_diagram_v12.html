Climate–Nature Governance Explorer

An interactive map of how UK and international climate- and nature-related governance connects: the legislation, public bodies, policies/activities, international instruments and indicator frameworks that make up the system, and the typed links between them. It is built directly from the three canonical workbooks, so it always reflects their current version.

What you're looking at

Records are grouped into horizontal tiers (international treaties → international bodies/EU law → UK legislation → UK public bodies → UK policy & activity → indicator frameworks). Colour shows the record's primary policy sector. Pick a record and the map shows everything it links to.

Reading the links

Line style shows link type; the arrow points from the governing entity to what it governs — enabling legislation → the body/policy it empowers, lead organisation → the policy it leads, parent → child, funder → funded.

  • Solid = hard links (enabling legislation, lead org/dept, parent/child).
  • Dashed = soft links (related / cross-reference, funded-by).
  • Double line = UK↔international bridge (solid = ratified/retained; dotted = thematic).
  • Solid pink = key instrument — the legislation, bodies and policies named in an indicator framework’s policy purpose.
  • Dotted = indicator / monitoring (softer cross-references).

Click any link type in the top legend to show/hide it.

How to use it

  • Find a record — search box or the list on the left; or click any node.
  • Expand — click a neighbour's + to add its own links (depth-2).
  • Hover a node to see its full name; the bottom panel shows the selected record's details and narrative.
  • Controls (top-right of the map): navigate/zoom/fit, display options (collapse depth, toggle Soft links, toggle EU law), and export (SVG/PNG of the current view).
  • More room — the header's ◧ Filters and ▤ Details buttons hide the side and bottom panels to enlarge the diagram (handy on laptops).
  • Deep-link — the URL ends with #RECORD-ID; share it to reopen on that record.

Record IDs: LEG = UK legislation · ORG = UK public body · POL = UK policy/activity · INT-L/O/P = international treaty/body/policy · EU-L = EU law · IFW = indicator framework.
